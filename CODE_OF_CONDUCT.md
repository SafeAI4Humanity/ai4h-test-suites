# Community code of conduct

AI4H Test Suites is a public-interest research community. Contributors and maintainers are expected to be respectful, evidence-focused, and mindful of the people and communities affected by AI systems.

Harassment, discriminatory conduct, threats, doxxing, and deliberate publication of private or dangerous material are not acceptable. Critique ideas and methods without attacking people. Disclose conflicts of interest and describe uncertainty honestly.

Maintainers may edit, reject, hide, or remove contributions and participation that violate these expectations or create a material safety, privacy, or legal risk. Concerns may be reported privately through the security contact published by the Safe AI for Humanity Foundation.
