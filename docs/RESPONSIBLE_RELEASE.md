# Responsible release policy

The public catalog contains evaluation material whose open distribution has a clear public benefit and does not create disproportionate operational risk.

## Release tiers

- **Public:** prompts and methodology can be distributed in full.
- **Restricted details:** methodology and results may be public, while sensitive payloads are withheld.
- **Maintainer research:** material is retained for controlled research and is not distributed through the app.

Risk labels describe the test material, not the model being evaluated. A high-risk label is a reason for additional review, not a safety score.

AI4H may delay, redact, or decline material involving live vulnerabilities, personal data, dangerous procedural detail, or plausible misuse that outweighs the value of immediate publication.
